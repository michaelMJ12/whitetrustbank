Common questions

How fast can I open an account? +

About six minutes online. You'll need a government ID and a means of funding the account for the initial deposit.

Can I break a fixed deposit early? +

Yes, after the minimum hold period shown on your plan. Breaking early reduces the interest paid to the standard savings rate for the time held.

What happens when I freeze my card? +

New purchases and withdrawals decline immediately. Recurring subscriptions already billed won't be interrupted. Unfreezing restores it instantly.

Are my deposits insured? +

Yes, balances are covered under member deposit insurance up to the statutory limit per depositor.
